Battly Overlay 1.0.1 adapts the LAN invitation and Worlds panel to small game windows.

The overlay uses slim themed scrollbars, tighter spacing at small sizes and a compact invitation in short windows. Room closure notices are displayed when the host closes a shared world.

Packages are available for Windows x86/x64, Linux x64/ARM64 and macOS Intel/Apple Silicon. Requires a compatible Battly host; fullscreen and window-manager limitations remain unchanged.
